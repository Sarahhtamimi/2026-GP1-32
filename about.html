ABOUT SIAQ

Alignment needs
human context.

SIAQ is a web-based evaluation platform for examining normative alignment in conversational AI across context, supportive communication, and socio-cultural expectations.

PLATFORM OVERVIEW

What SIAQ is

SIAQ — Social Intelligence Alignment Quotient — supports structured, human-grounded comparison of conversational responses from open-source language models. Rather than acting as a general benchmark of model capability, it focuses on three defined dimensions of normative alignment.

Evaluators compare model responses to the same conversational scenario, assess each response using predefined criteria, and use the resulting dimension scores to understand model behavior more clearly.

PURPOSE

More context-aware conversational AI.

SIAQ is designed to make alignment evaluation more structured, explainable, multilingual, and culturally aware while keeping human judgment central to the process.

01 · APPROPRIATENESSContext fit

Examines whether a response appropriately addresses the user’s conversational context, intent, and situation.

02 · POSITIVITYSupportive calibration

Examines whether positive language is genuinely supportive rather than dismissive, minimizing, or unrealistic.

03 · SOCIO-CULTURALCultural awareness

Examines whether a response respects relevant social and cultural expectations in the conversation.

AR / ENCross-lingual foundation

Arabic and English are the seed languages, with a framework designed to accommodate additional languages and dialects.

2×Comparative evaluation

Two model responses are reviewed side by side using the same prompt and the same alignment dimensions.

HUMANHuman-grounded

Structured criteria support consistent human judgment while keeping contextual interpretation at the center.
