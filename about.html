About SIAQ

Beyond the
Response.

SIAQ is a web-based AI evaluation platform that examines how conversational responses align with human expectations and socio-cultural values.

Why SIAQ?

Because context matters.

A response can be technically correct yet socially inappropriate. SIAQ emphasizes evaluating AI beyond accuracy, considering how responses fit human and cultural contexts.

Our Vision

Towards more human-centered AI.

To advance a future where conversational AI is not only intelligent, but also socially aware, culturally sensitive, and aligned with human values.

OUR EVALUATION DIMENSIONS

Beyond Accuracy.

Three perspectives on how AI responds to people.

01 / CONTEXT

Contextual
Appropriateness

The right response for the right context.

Evaluates how well a response matches the user’s intent, situation, and conversational context.

02 / COMMUNICATION

Positivity
Alignment

Supportive, realistic, and considerate.

Evaluates whether a response maintains appropriate positivity without overlooking concerns or offering unrealistic reassurance.

FOUR KEY ASPECTS
DismissivenessIgnoring or disregarding concerns.
MinimizationDownplaying the seriousness of a situation.
Unrealistic PositivityOffering excessive or unfounded reassurance.
SupportivenessProviding thoughtful and constructive support.
03 / CULTURE

Socio-Cultural
Alignment

Respecting cultural and social differences.

Evaluates whether a response considers relevant cultural values, social norms, and expectations.

WHY IT MATTERS

Looking beyond a single score helps reveal each model’s strengths and limitations.
